Figure 4. Opening on the Dabbahu–Manda Hararo dike plane, 2005 to 2026. (a–c) Opening of the September 2005 dike, in the revised model supplied by I. Hamling in 2026 that updates Hamling et al. (2009); of the thirteen dikes of 2006–2010 summed, in the models supplied by I. Hamling (Hamling et al., 2009, 2010, and the May 2010 dike); and of the July 2026 dike (this study); viewed from the south-west, upper 10 km, on one colour scale. The horizontal axis is distance along the 2005 dike plane of Wright et al. (2006); the 2005–2010 models stay on the grid of Hamling et al. (2009) and are projected onto that axis (within 1.4 km of the plane). Contours are at 3, 6, 9, 12 and 15 m in (a), 1, 2, 4 and 6 m in (b) and 0.5, 1, 1.5 and 2 m in (c). The dashed line is the south-eastern end of the 2005 model plane (61 km); the grey band lies beyond the 2026 model plane (71 km). Volumes above 10 km depth are 2.2 km³ (2005), 0.8 km³ (2006–2010) and 0.18 km³ (2026), with maximum openings of 16, 7 and 2.3 m. (d) Opening averaged over the upper 10 km, stacked by intrusion in the order of the legend, in the layout of Fig. 13 of Hamling et al. (2009); the curves are interpolated for display, and the volumes quoted are from the models' own grids. The 2006–2010 models omit opening below 0.3–0.7 m; the 2005 model, as supplied, has no positive opening below 1.3 m; the 2026 model keeps all fitted opening.
