# Ascending/descending East–Up decomposition

This two-component solution assumes zero north displacement and is reported as
quasi-east/quasi-up rather than full 3-D displacement. Statistics
below are within 18 km of the analysis centre.

- East p01–p99: -59.0 to +63.1 cm
- Up p01–p99: -18.1 to +37.3 cm
